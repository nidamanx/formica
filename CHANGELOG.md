# Changelog

All notable changes to Formica are documented in this file.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/),
and this project adheres to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [Unreleased]

### Changed

- About: the app icon, name and version at the top, and every section in its own box.
- About opens from the ⋮ menu, next to Settings, instead of from the settings.
- The permission that keeps the phone awake is explained better: it is used only during
  the daily check and does not turn on the screen.
- The notification icon has the same closed smile as the app icon.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-25

### Added

- Pantry: products with quantities (decimals too), units, categories, locations and
  notes; several packs with their own expiry dates, "opened on" and the days a product
  keeps after opening, all editable later; search, filters and sorting.
- Homemade products and products from producers: preparation date, the day a
  preparation is ready, purchases in large quantities.
- Estimated duration of the stock, from the use of the last weeks.
- Product history with the latest moves; a purchase or a preparation entered by mistake
  can be undone.
- Shopping list: items linked to the pantry or free text, minimum stock, products
  running low and bought often, grouped by category or shop; cheapest shop and price
  for each product.
- Expiry dates: expired, expiring soon and getting ready; one daily notification at the
  chosen time.
- Data exchange with the family through a file: preview, three import modes (merge, new
  products only, replace everything), merge of the changes made on several phones,
  backup copy before every import, undo, exchange log, clean-up of deleted items.
- Welcome screen at the first start, About screen.
- Light and dark theme in the colours of the icon; TalkBack descriptions and layouts for
  large text.
- English, Italian, French, German and Spanish.
