Formica keeps the household pantry and the shopping list in order, and shares them with the family by exchanging a file between phones. It works without internet, without an account and without a server.

<b>Pantry</b>
• Products with quantities (decimals too), units, category, location and notes
• Several packs of the same product, with different expiry dates
• "Opened on", with the days it keeps after opening
• An estimate of how long your stock will last

<b>Homemade and from producers</b>
• Preserves, yogurt, fermented food and many others, with the preparation date
• The day a preparation is ready
• Purchases in large quantities from producers

<b>Shopping list</b>
• Items linked to the pantry or free text
• Minimum stock: below the amount you choose, the product goes on the list by itself
• Products bought often, grouped by category or by shop
• Best price: the shop and the best price for each product

<b>Expiry dates</b>
• One notification a day with what expires and what is ready

<b>In the family</b>
• Export a file and open it on another phone: changes are merged
• Preview and backup before every import, which can be undone

<b>Privacy</b>
• No internet access, no account, no ads or trackers
• Android's automatic backup turned off: data leaves the phone only when you export it
• Free software (AGPL-3.0)
